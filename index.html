Good to know

How do I share photos from iPhone to Android?

Open PhotoCrew in the browser on both phones, start a room on one and scan its QR code with the other. Add your photos and the other phone downloads the originals directly, at full quality. No app to install and no account.

Is it like AirDrop?

Similar idea, but it works across every device, not just Apple ones, and with a whole group at once. Everyone sees one shared grid and picks only the photos they want.

Do photos lose quality?

No. You download the exact original files your friends added, not compressed copies like many chat apps send.

Do we need the same Wi-Fi?

No, but it works best that way. If the Wi-Fi keeps devices apart, as guest networks often do, join one person's hotspot. Connecting over the internet works on many networks, though not all.

Does it work without internet?

No. You need a connection to open PhotoCrew and for devices to find each other. After that, photos travel directly between them.

Who can see what I add?

Anyone with the room's link or code can join and see it, so share the code only with people you trust. When you leave, your originals are no longer available.

What can relay servers see?

Connection details, such as your IP address, so devices can reach each other. People in your room connect to you directly, so they can learn it too. Photos don't go through the relays.

What happens if someone leaves?

Their previews stay in your grid, marked offline. You can't download their originals until they come back and add them again, so keep your tab open until everyone's done.

Can I share videos or hundreds of photos?

PhotoCrew is for photos only. Downloads are held in memory until you save them, so take big sets in smaller batches.
